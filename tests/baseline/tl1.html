WEEBLY

Relationships
Weebly is a free, easy-to-use website builder. It lets you create and publish attractive sites, blogs, and online stores. For any subject area. Some of these tools can help give you insight into students' lives and thinking, helping you build those positive relationships. Please note: this product needs a VPN. Please ensure all students have access before using this tool.

PHET

Teacher PlanningModify their Teaching
PhET provides free, fun, interactive, research-based science and mathematics simulations for grades K-12 and college. To help students visually comprehend concepts, PhET simulations animate what is invisible to the eye through the use of graphics and intuitive controls such as click-and-drag manipulations, sliders and radio buttons. The software can be modified to address student questions or meet student needs.

ZOOM

Modify their Teaching
Use Zoom breakout rooms to adapt teaching to meet students' needs. Meet with students in groups or individually. A few students have trouble understanding a concept? Meet with that group separately while the rest of the class does other work in a separate meeting room. (Please note: Zoom is currently unavailable in China except through a VPN. Please be careful using this - ensure all students will have access! Consider using Zhumu.)
  • 2-minute video on how to setup breakout rooms, Educational focus, not business

GIMKIT

Achieve Readiness
​Gimkit is a classroom game-show platform where students compete by answering questions on their electronic devices. Instead of earning points, students earn virtual currency, which they can "invest" during the game to boost their score. Teachers check students' understanding and adjust their teaching as needed. Similar to Kahoot.

SEESAW

RelationshipsAchieve Readiness
Seesaw is a student-driven digital portfolio that inspires your students to do their best work. It also allows teachers and students to share that work with families. Students can show what they know using various media, like photos, videos, drawings, text, and hyperlinks. Not only does this tool enable positive relationships, it gives the teacher a window into student knowledge and progress so the teacher can adjust accordingly.

PADLET

Relationships
Padlet is a digital tool that can help teachers and students by offering a single place for an online notice board. It allows teachers and students to curate information onto virtual boards that allow users to easily express their thoughts on a common topic and read what others have to say. It is a perfect way to understand your students' thinking and build positive relationships with them.

GRADECAM

Teacher PlanningModify their Teaching
GradeCam allows teachers to use their device's camera to instantly scan and grade multiple-choice, fill-in, or short answer assessments. In short, it's a new take on the old Scantron machine but with a number of more useful features. Fast results lead to an understanding of students' progress, allowing you to adjust as necessary.

KAHOOT

Teacher Planning
Kahoot! is a game-based learning platform. "Kahoots" are multiple-choice quizzes that can be accessed via a web browser or the Kahoot! app. Kahoot! can be used to review students' knowledge for formative assessment or as a supplement to or in lieu of traditional classroom activities. Once the teacher has the data about a students' progress, the teacher can adjust their teaching to meet students' needs.

QUIZLET

Teacher Planning
Quizlet is a study aid in app form. In essence, it's a flashcard app with smart features, and it can handle images, diagrams, various languages, and even audio uploads. It's ideal for self-paced, rote-style learning. You can make your own study sets, get access to study sets created by instructors, or browse for sets created by other users. It allows students to work at their own pace and teachers to give the students what they need to be successful.

Teacher Planning
Quizizz is a learning platform that uses gamification to make content engaging. Participants can engage in live and asynchronous learning using any device in-person or remotely. Teachers get instant data and feedback while learners engage with gamification features in fun, competitive quizzes and interactive presentations. The data can help the teacher modify their teaching to meet students' needs.
