How does IPstress work?

Pick a plan that fits the capacity you need, top up your balance, and launch tests from the Hub. Our backends handle the routing and concurrency so you can focus on the target.

What payment methods do you accept?

Stripe, PayPal, and Bitcoin. Crypto payments credit automatically once one confirmation lands. Card payments are instant.

How long does support take?

Tickets are answered around the clock. Most replies land within 20 minutes during business hours and within 2 hours overnight.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are non-recurring by default — they expire at the end of the term. If a plan is unusable due to a fault on our side, we issue a prorated refund to your balance.

Is my account safe?

All launches are routed through isolated infrastructure. We do not log target metadata, and your payment details are tokenised by the gateway — we never store raw card data.
