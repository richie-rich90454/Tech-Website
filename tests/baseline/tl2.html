Multi-dimensional LearningRepertoire of Techniques
Weebly is a free, easy-to-use website builder. It lets you create and publish attractive sites, blogs, and online stores. For any subject area. Great for individual or group work, so it adds to your repertoire of techniques. And, it has a multi-dimensional aspect as well. Please note: this product needs a VPN. PHET

Multi-dimensional LearningRepertoire of Techniques
PhET provides free, fun, interactive, research-based science and mathematics simulations for grades K-12 and college. To help students visually comprehend concepts, PhET simulations animate what is invisible to the eye through the use of graphics and intuitive controls such as click-and-drag manipulations, sliders and radio buttons. This software will add a multi-dimensional tool to your repertoire of techniques.

Group work can be facilitated by using the Channel feature in Microsoft Teams. As part of your repertoire of techniques, Teams can be used to facilitate classroom discussion online. QUIZIZZ

Understanding
Quizizz is a learning platform that uses gamification to make content engaging. Participants can engage in live and asynchronous learning using any device-in person or remotely. Teachers get instant data and feedback while learners engage with gamification features in fun, competitive quizzes and interactive presentations. The data serves as a check for understanding before moving ahead with new topics. Further, the platform adds a multi-dimensional element to instruction.
